## Feasibility and design established before implementation

Resolved RuneLite 1.13.3 exposes slot, item ID, buy/sell state, requested quantity/limit price, cumulative executed quantity and cumulative reported GP through GrandExchangeOfferChanged/GrandExchangeOffer. Price and spent are long. getGrandExchangeOffers supplies eight current snapshots, not completed historical trades. The event Javadoc explicitly documents transient EMPTY events at login. The official core GrandExchangePlugin ignores non-LOGGED_IN clears, retains saved offer signatures and compares cumulative quantity/GP. From Scratch implements its own reducer and durable generation IDs.

There is no server offer UUID, reliable execution timestamp, cost basis, collection destination or complete offline history in those fields. Identical slot reuse during absence can be indistinguishable. Initial snapshots and discontinuities must retain uncertainty rather than fabricate a sequence. EMPTY is a cleared slot, not proof of cancellation or bank collection. No GE visit is required for bank tracking.

Bank P/B remain exclusively driven by complete bank observations. GE records never apply an independent item/coin delta. A bounded attribution layer consumes compatible trade/movement budgets once and labels these links possible, not verified provenance: identical copies cannot be distinguished without tracking inventory. Potential conversion portions are conservatively excluded from new bank high-water gains, while still raising the ceiling. This avoids treating ordinary observed buys/sales/returns as fresh income. Unrelated item IDs remain eligible; unmatched and late evidence stays uncertain, without rewriting M5 history. Current worth remains bank-only, uncollected assets excluded and realized profit unknown.

GE state and immutable per-operation evidence join the existing forced journal/checkpoint/current chain in schema 7. M5 originals must be verified and archived byte-for-byte before migration; identity/start/P/B/initial snapshot/financial history remain unchanged. Offer and bank observations share the serial worker and FIFO acknowledgment path. Price requests, rendering and UI never perform GE disk work. Histories are bounded pages from immutable journal segments.

## Tax sources and limits

Jagex's [29 May 2025 update](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/yama-cas--more?oldschool=1) establishes 2% for new sell offers, rounded-down small amounts, exemptions and the retained five-million cap; older offers can retain 1%. The [30 September 2026 update](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/ge-improvements-beyond-max-cash?oldschool=1) confirms the per-item cap and coins/platinum settlement. Long-valued API totals must be preserved. No buyer tax or second bank deduction is allowed.

The public offer API does not document a tax amount or whether seller spent includes/excludes fees. Named GE_TAX_SLOT_LONG varps and GE_TRANSMIT_TAXRATE exist, but their synchronization/semantics are not documented by the supported offer API; From Scratch will not label them verified. Execution GP is explicitly API-reported; tax based on average price is an estimate, not a claim of per-unit fill prices, verified net proceeds or profit. The [original Jagex announcement](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/grand-exchange-tax--item-sink?oldschool=1) supplies the explicit tool/bond exemptions; Mind runes are named in the 2025 update. The source policy includes those 15 verified item IDs, not guessed food/ammo categories. Other item exemptions and old rates may make a displayed estimate differ. Unknown/preexisting fee history is not reconstructed.

## Implemented behavior

- Public offer packets are copied immediately and queued to the existing background worker. Current nonempty offer snapshots seed observation after login; missing/null data has no effect on ordinary banking. Initial EMPTY is ignored. No GE screen, menu input, personal container or review wizard is needed.
- Character/run/slot-local generations, offer signature, cumulative quantities/GP, connection context and provenance persist. Record only additional fills. Status/cleared slots do not invent executions, cancellation reasons or collection destinations. A matching unchanged resume refreshes session metadata without duplicating history; later clears/reuse remain observable. Backward terminal-state packets cannot recreate refunds. Pre-start cached offers use explicit lifecycle evidence with their real original timestamps.
- Buy items, sale GP, unspent buy cash and unsold returns get finite possible-match budgets. Compatible withdrawals and later deposits consume units once. Concurrent same-item offers cannot consume the same deposit twice. Coins/platinum share a GP budget; no synthetic currency or inventory wallet exists. Matching references and uncertainty are durable audit data; the history details conservatively state collection unknown, rather than presenting those associations as verified collection.
- Possible conversion portions are excluded from new high-water gains and still raise H. A purchased item deposit or sale-proceeds deposit never applies an additional ownership/GP delta. Unrelated IDs and amounts beyond compatible budgets remain eligible. Tax is counted once in the estimated-fee statistic, without a second bank deduction. Cost basis/profit stay unknown.
- All GE and bank writes use one serial forced journal/checkpoint/current transaction stream. Only acknowledged results update UI/renderer. CAS, identity/run/context checks, archive verification and recovery evidence remain. Extreme bursts retain the latest cumulative value per slot with a history-gap explanation instead of filling the bank queue. Counts summarize observed fill records, not a fabricated server order count.
- Purchases/Sales tiles sit between Bank History and Played Elsewhere, using cached COINS (995) / PLATINUM (13204) sprites. Manual history remains Diary (3846); Manual Bank Corrections remains Purple partyhat (1046). The six metric tiles, setup checkbox/green Start, reset safeguards, original quantities and accepted bank geometry/tint are preserved. GE-only commits reuse the immutable bank-label cache.
- Read-only history loads at most 25 rows/64 segments per request. It shows item sprite/name, quantity, reported execution GP, derived average, clearly estimated tax, status and local observed time. Page-local name search, activity/uncertainty filters and numeric sorting are available. Exact details explain unknown profit, provenance, gross/net basis and collection destination. Item labels disable Swing HTML parsing. No scrolling feed is added to the dashboard.

## Persistence and migration

Format **7** requires explicit GE checkpoint data. Each GE or attributed-bank operation embeds immutable input, trade, match and exclusion evidence; persistence independently recomputes the expected GE and financial transitions. Current state bounds are eight offers, 256 pending credits, 128 unmatched withdrawals and 30 recent rows. Full older evidence stays in the linked journal; normal changes never reconstruct the full ledger.

Before format-6 migration, verify the complete original chain; copy every original JSON checkpoint/segment byte-for-byte into a forced, hash-verified manifest archive; append EXCHANGE_BASELINE; then publish current atomically/recoverably. Run ID/start/initial legacy bank/P/B/financial high-water/quotes/totals/history remain unchanged. No historical trades, gains or fees are reconstructed. A matching existing M5 run needs no bank anchor. Older formats retain prior migrations and archive safeguards. Missing/corrupt/newer/incomplete state is rejected without empty defaults or deletion. Interrupted and lost-acknowledgment tests retain the committed checkpoint and recover without duplication. Back up/restore the entire character directory, including all older archive folders and linked journals, while RuneLite is closed.

## Known limits

1. There is no server offer UUID, execution timestamp, cost basis or collection-destination field. Clear means cleared, not banked. Partial collection is only a possible subsequent bank match, never a verified inventory/bank collection history. Offline identical reuse can be indistinguishable and is marked conditional; a whole trade created/collected/removed elsewhere is not recoverable.
2. A first-seen already-filled offer is an existing cumulative snapshot, even if it might have just filled. Without a prior observation the plugin cannot assert those earlier executions happened during this run. Offer origin can remain unknown. Native GE packets arriving after an already committed bank gain cannot safely rewrite that earlier financial history; they remain unclassified for that earlier operation. Missing/expired/bounded matching context can therefore overstate estimated conversion gains; possible same-ID matching can understate unrelated gains. The statistic remains an estimate, not profit or perfect income.
3. Seller tax is estimated, never silently presented as observed. Additional 2025 food/ammo/other exemptions are not fully enumerated by the public offer API. API seller gross/net semantics, per-unit prices inside a batch and old offer rates are unverified. Fees can differ from native tax; compare the native GE display during acceptance. Buyer tax is not applied, and current bank proceeds remain the actual physical bank observation regardless of fee estimates.
4. Match windows last 24 hours and are capacity-bounded; older records remain readable but no longer imply automatic attribution. Late/nonmatching transformations and unobserved GP conversions do not acquire invented provenance. Current bank value excludes all out-of-bank commitments and uncollected assets.
5. Known preexisting GE returns do not silently change L or override the bank equation. A concise optional Manual Bank Corrections notice explains the legacy offset; no normal review gate is added. No inventory/equipment walkthrough or forced GE visit occurs after offline play.

## Manual acceptance

1. Preserve `v0.5.0-wealth` and back up the complete local character directory with RuneLite closed. Launch with `.\gradlew.bat run` from the repository. For Jagex Accounts use the official [development-login instructions](https://github.com/runelite/runelite/wiki/Using-Jagex-Accounts). Only the user performs game actions.
2. Log into the existing accepted run. Confirm no reset/new bank-anchor/GE setup screen appears. Open the bank only: signed quantities, Bank GP, current worth, history and earlier gained/expense totals must match before migration. Verify an exact-byte migration archive exists locally; never post private saves publicly.
3. Manually withdraw a known coin amount, place a small ordinary buy offer, allow fills, collect and bank items/refund. Native bank changes must happen normally; purple quantity changes exactly once on physical deposit, and Bank GP follows only physical coins. Uncollected items must not appear in bank worth. Purchase history must show incremental quantities/reported GP, average and observed time, with no buyer fee. Unknown initial snapshots must be labelled rather than inventing history.
4. Withdraw known bank items, sell them, collect and bank the proceeds. Purple item count decreases only through the withdrawal; coin progress increases only on physical deposit. Estimated Value Gained must not add the compatible sale proceeds as fresh gains. Sales history records the observed execution total and labels tax estimated; compare against the native GE tax/net display. Total Lost/Spent adds the estimate once without another Bank GP deduction. Profit remains unknown.
5. Exercise partial buy/sell fills, then cancel. Collect remaining items/refund in separate portions and bank them. Verify each physical change is counted once, no duplicated fills/fees after collection, and returns do not create new high-water gains. Use two offers for the same item to verify one deposit cannot satisfy both budgets in full.
6. Withdraw/redeposit bought items below the current high-water ceiling; gained value must not repeat. Deposit an unrelated item or an amount above the remaining compatible budget; only that eligible additional progression can add fixed-price gains. Price refresh must not rewrite history. Same-ID provenance is possible rather than verified, as explained above.
7. Restart or hop with an active partially filled offer. Confirm saved generation/cumulative quantities persist, initial EMPTY does not erase them and identical resumed amounts do not create another execution. After a later clear, reuse the slot; a newly observed placement must get a new local generation. Ambiguous same-signature/offline reuse must stay labelled uncertain.
8. For a separate unstarted test character, observe an existing offer before starting, then perform the usual bank-only attestation/Start. Do not reset the accepted run. After collecting/banking that preexisting offer, verify no fresh estimated gain for the compatible return and the optional legacy-count correction notice. Cancel any unneeded manual correction.
9. After playing elsewhere, bank items and open the bank on this PC. Quantities update automatically without inventory/equipment/GE walkthroughs. Compatible saved-offer net changes may be recorded with uncertainty; entirely unobserved completed trades must not be fabricated.
10. Inspect both new tiles and paginated history. Search/sort/filter, select a row for exact details, go Previous/Next/Close/Escape, resize and test display scaling. Check Diary remains history-only and Purple partyhat remains the correction tool. Main dashboard stays two-column and reset stays last.
11. Compare native bank responsiveness enabled/disabled during rapid banking, with 1,000+ items if available. Check counters after durable acknowledgment, partial top/bottom rows, scroll/search/tabs, dragging/menus, negative placeholders, custom colors and disable/re-enable. Synthetic CPU/IO timings cannot establish native input/server latency.
