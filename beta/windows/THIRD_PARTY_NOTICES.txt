lib/ contains unmodified official RuneLite Maven artifacts and their normal
transitive runtime dependencies, plus the separately identified From Scratch
plugin and beta bootstrap. These are separate JARs, not a modified RuneLite
client or a merged/shaded runtime. JUnit and historical test fixtures are not
distributed. Java is a separately installed prerequisite, not bundled.

DEPENDENCIES.json records exact artifact coordinates and SHA-256 hashes.
Upstream copyright/license/NOTICE files inside each dependency JAR remain
unchanged. Their licenses continue to apply. In particular, RuneLite uses
BSD 2-Clause: https://github.com/runelite/runelite/blob/master/LICENSE
